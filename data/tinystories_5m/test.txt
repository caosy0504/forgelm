He opened the gift and saw a small box inside. The box had a button on it. Tim pushed the button and something unexpected happened. The big rock started to move! It was not a rock, it was a big turtle!
The turtle said, "Thank you for finding my gift, Tim!" The turtle was very nice. From that day on, Tim and the turtle became best friends. They played together in the woods every day.
